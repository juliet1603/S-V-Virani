About this item 

    The Lava Blaze Amoled 2 is a 5G budget smartphone featuring a 6.67-inch 120Hz AMOLED display, a MediaTek Dimensity 7060 processor, and a 5000mAh battery. It launched on August 11, 2025, starting at an introductory price of Rs. 13,499 for the 6GB RAM and 128GB storage variant. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]  
Display and Design 

• Size: 16.94 cm (6.67 inches) FHD+ punch-hole AMOLED panel. 
• Quality: 120Hz refresh rate with 10-bit color support. 
• Body size: 163.2 x 76.05 x 7.55 mm. 
• Weight: 174 grams. 
• Colors: Midnight Black and Feather White. [1, 3, 6]  

Performance and Hardware 

• Chipset: Octa-core MediaTek Dimensity 7060 (6nm). 
• Memory: 6GB RAM with expandable virtual RAM support. 
• Storage: 128GB internal storage (expandable up to 1TB via microSD). [1, 2]  

Cameras and Battery 

• Rear camera: 50MP main sensor plus a secondary QVGA/AI lens. 
• Front camera: 8MP selfie shooter. 
• Battery capacity: 5000mAh. 
• Charging: 33W fast charging support (0-50% in under 35 minutes). [1, 7, 8, 9, 10]
